⚙️ How it works

From idea to delivery in 3 steps

HireMint takes the friction out of finding, hiring and paying freelancers — for clients and freelancers alike.

For clients

Post a project and meet matched talent within a day.

Post your project

Describe the work, budget and timeline. Our system parses your brief and finds the right experts.

Match with talent

Get a shortlist of pre-vetted freelancers within 24 hours, ranked by fit, rating and availability.

Hire & collaborate

Chat, share files and pay securely. Funds are released only when you're happy with the work.

For freelancers

Get verified, then land work that matches your skills.

Create your profile

Sign up, fill in your skills, experience and rate. Get verified instantly upon registration.

Activate your account

Purchase the HireMint Freelancer Handbook ($49) — a one-time activation that unlocks the platform.

Get matched & paid

Clients invite you to jobs that fit your expertise. Get paid securely through HireMint Escrow.
